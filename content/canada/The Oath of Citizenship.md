I swear (or affirm)  
That I will be faithful  
And bear true allegiance  
To His Majesty  
King Charles the Third  
King of Canada  
His Heirs and Successors  
And that I will faithfully observe  
The laws of Canada  
Including the Constitution  
Which recognizes and affirms  
The Aboriginal and treaty rights of  
First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples  
And fulfil my duties as a Canadian citizen.[^1]

Canada swears loyalty to a person representing all Canadians. In its [[Constitutional monarchy]], that person is the Sovereign (The King).[^1]

[^1]: [The Oath of Citizenship](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/discover-canada/read-online/oath-citizenship.html), Discover Canada

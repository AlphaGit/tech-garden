They are commonly used mathematical techniques to solve constrained optimization problems. 

In this example, the optimization is to be done onto $\max f(x)$ with the constraint $g(x) = 0$.

$$
\begin{gather*}
\max f(x) ; g(x) = 0 \\
L(x,\lambda) = f(x) - \lambda * g(x)
\end{gather*}
$$
